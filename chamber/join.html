NP Membership

For non profit organizations, at no cost.

  • Listing in the member directory
  • Access to community events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter

Bronze Membership

Ideal for new and small businesses.

  • Everything in NP
  • 10% discount on chamber events
  • One business training session per year
  • Basic profile in the directory

Silver Membership

For growing businesses.

  • Everything in Bronze
  • 25% discount on chamber events
  • Four training sessions per year
  • Eligible for home page spotlight advertising
  • Quarterly business networking

Gold Membership

Maximum visibility and benefits.

  • Everything in Silver
  • Free admission to all chamber events
  • Unlimited training sessions
  • Priority placement in spotlights and directory
  • Featured advertising in the newsletter
  • One on one business consulting
